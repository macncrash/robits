# Sun gear (m1, 18 T)

STEP: [`step/sun.step`](step/sun.step) (teeth are the reference shape, with the 0.38 m root fillet). Source:
`source/components/sun.py` and `source/gearing.py`, design revision 48.

**Who makes it:** a gear shop. The CNC shop's part is the blank (turn OD, faces and a pilot bore, then send for
hobbing); the gear shop hobs, carburises, grinds the teeth, and wire-EDMs the double-D bore after hardening. Most
gear shops prefer to do the whole part from their own certified bar. See also `../hardware/gears.md`.

## Notes

- **Thin rim:** only 1.75 mm of steel between the bore and the tooth root (1.75 m). It is enough for this
  load (bending 143 MPa with the root fillet, case-hardened), but make sure the heat treater keeps distortion
  in check and checks the rim for through-hardening.
- Inspection: gear report per ISO 1328 (profile, helix, pitch, runout), W3, hardness and case depth on a
  coupon from the same batch.

| Teeth can't be milled on a 3-axis machine | They are hobbed and ground by a gear shop to the table above; the CAD teeth are a reference |
| Bore had no drive feature | Double-D, wire-EDM |
