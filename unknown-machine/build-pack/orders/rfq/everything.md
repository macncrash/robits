# Planetary joint actuator: complete build pack

This zip is the whole build pack.

- `cnc/`: drawings, STEP files and process sheets for the machined parts.
- `hardware/`: the BOM, sourcing decisions, the drive interface, gears, stock and the cost estimate.
- `pcb/`: the control pendant board.
- `print/`: its printed enclosure.
- `ASSEMBLY.md` and `TEST.md`: how to put it together and power it up.

The per-vendor request texts are in `orders/rfq/`.

Nothing in here has been ordered. Each vendor packet (cnc-shop, gear-shop, jlcpcb-pendant, print-pendant,
hardware) is also available on its own.
