# 3D printing: pendant enclosure (2 parts)

| File | Part | Material | Orientation | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| pendant-tub.stl | Tub | PETG | as exported (+Z up; one 14 mm bridge over the USB slot) | 0.2 mm layers, 4 walls, 30 % infill |
| pendant-lid.stl | Lid | PETG | as exported (face down) | Same settings |

Hardware to go with it:
- M3 brass heat-set inserts;
- M3 screws;
- a cable gland for the pendant cable.

The enclosure design is in STEVE: https://stevecad.studio/?document=4032528e-7594-4bb4-b2b7-6de9d52228b1, revision 11.

Print it yourself, or upload the STLs to any print service (JLC3DP, Craftcloud, Xometry). Quantity: 1 per pendant.
